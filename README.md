# AI Commerce Intelligence Framework

The AI Commerce Intelligence Framework is a model for understanding how AI systems discover, interpret, trust, compare, recommend, and route customers to businesses.

Traditional commerce was optimized for search.

AI-native commerce is optimized for recommendation.

---

## The AI Commerce Intelligence Layers

### 1. AI Readability

Can AI systems access and parse your information?

Signals:

- Structured data
- Crawlability
- Product feeds
- Machine-readable content
- Technical accessibility

---

### 2. AI Understanding

Can AI systems understand what you sell and who you serve?

Signals:

- Product clarity
- Category definition
- Entity consistency
- Semantic structure
- Contextual relevance

---

### 3. AI Trust

Can AI systems trust the claims made by your business?

Signals:

- Reviews
- Reputation
- Third-party mentions
- Customer feedback
- Evidence-backed claims

---

### 4. Recommendation Visibility

How often does your brand appear in AI-generated recommendations?

Signals:

- Citation frequency
- Recommendation frequency
- Competitive comparison presence
- Authority indicators

---

### 5. Recommendation Confidence

How confident is an AI system when recommending your business?

Signals:

- Data consistency
- Market reputation
- Product quality signals
- Knowledge completeness

---

## AI Commerce Score

The AI Commerce Score measures a company's ability to be discovered, understood, trusted, and recommended by AI systems.

AI Commerce Score =

Readability +
Understanding +
Trust +
Visibility +
Confidence
